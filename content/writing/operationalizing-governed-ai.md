---

Organizations do not realize value from AI merely by selecting a model. Sustainable value requires a disciplined operating system: clearly defined business outcomes, high-quality data, secure architecture, model evaluation, ownership, monitoring, and escalation paths.

A governed AI program should measure both value and risk. Value metrics may include cycle-time reduction, decision quality, revenue impact, and adoption. Risk metrics should include model performance, security posture, data quality, bias indicators, exception volume, and human-review outcomes.

For generative and agentic systems, governance must be designed into the delivery lifecycle rather than added after deployment.
