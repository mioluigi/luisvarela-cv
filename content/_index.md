## Professional Summary

I help organizations turn AI ambition into secure, reliable systems that create business value. With more than 25 years of consulting and technology leadership experience across Fortune 50 and Fortune 100 enterprises, global service providers, telecommunications, financial institutions, pharmaceutical organizations, and startups, I connect executive strategy with architecture, engineering delivery, and operational governance.

My **differentiator** is understanding how modern AI systems are actually built, deployed, and abused. I bring that perspective to AI operationalization, cybersecurity, and Responsible AI across narrow AI, computer vision, generative AI, and agentic AI. I help clients identify valuable use cases, assess readiness, design secure cloud and data foundations, and establish the evaluation, deployment controls, monitoring, and human oversight needed to move from experimentation toward dependable operations.

As Chief Transformation Officer at OWNE Intelligence and President of the Global Institute of Data Science, I lead work at the intersection of AI architecture, security, and trust. My responsibilities include secure platform design, model and agent deployment governance, Responsible AI risk reviews, and controls that make AI-enabled decisions auditable and accountable. Earlier leadership roles at JPMorgan Chase, Accenture, and Cisco provide a foundation in enterprise-scale security, multidisciplinary delivery, and complex transformation.

I work with executive leaders, founders, and engineering teams as a strategic advisor, fractional technology and security leader, and consulting delivery partner. Whether the challenge is operationalizing AI, securing an agentic workflow, strengthening data governance, or evaluating an existing architecture, my focus is practical: align the technology with business priorities, establish clear ownership and safeguards, and build an actionable path to customer trust, operational resilience, and sustainable value.
