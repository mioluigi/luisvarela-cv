Responsible AI and Secure MLOps

Governing AI from experimentation to production

I advise organizations on the controls required to operate predictive, generative, and agentic AI systems responsibly.

Core practices

  • AI use-case risk assessment and prioritization
  • Data governance and model-governance frameworks
  • Model evaluation, monitoring, and performance thresholds
  • Secure MLOps and LLMOps operating practices
  • Human-in-the-loop escalation and review
  • AI security architecture, identity, access, and auditability
  • Executive KPIs for value, risk, adoption, and resilience

Outcome

The goal is not simply to deploy models. It is to create secure, measurable, accountable systems that can be trusted at enterprise scale.
